# Quality

Keep warnings as errors and source changes focused. Avoid unrelated refactors,
catch-all Ada exception suppression, copied headers and unexplained constants.
Explain conservative acceptance bounds and distinguish them from native limits.
Do not claim formal proof, real-time behavior, navigation accuracy or complete
cross-version safety from a build or synthetic fixture.

Update coverage, public contracts, architecture decisions and the validation
record with changes. Do not add placeholder success paths for missing features.
Keep config/obj/lib/bin caches, credentials and environment-specific absolute
paths out of committed source. Production dependency changes need justification.
